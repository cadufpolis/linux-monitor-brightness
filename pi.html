Dial name
Shown on the dial's screen. Leave blank to use the selected monitor's name (or "N displays" when more than one is picked). Saves when you click away or press Enter.
Monitors controlled by this dial
Pick one or more monitors. Rotating the dial adjusts all of them together; pressing it (or tapping the screen) toggles a quick dim.
Rotate delay
How long to wait, after you stop turning the dial, before the brightness change is actually sent to the monitor (ms). Higher is gentler on slower monitors. Saves when you click away or press Enter.
